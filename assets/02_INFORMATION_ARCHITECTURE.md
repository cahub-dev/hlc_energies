## 4. Page requirements

### About HLC

Use only the approved company description, legal data and corporate purpose from `COPY.md`. Legal identifiers may be hidden until publication approval.

### Areas of Interest

Present approved sectors as areas of interest, not completed HLC services or operational assets.

### Consortium

Explain the division of responsibilities and the client-facing contract position. Avoid vague partnership language.

### Technical Capacity

This page is explicitly about CC1. It must not visually imply the indicators belong to HLC.

### Reference Projects

Project cards and detail pages must show source, project facts and CC1 attribution. Do not add status, client, contract value or HLC role unless approved.

### Commitment to Mozambique

Use the approved four national-commitment pillars plus capacity-transfer content. Avoid unsupported ESG statistics.

### Contact

Form fields:

- Name / Nome
- Organization / Organização
- Email / E-mail
- Telephone / Telefone (optional)
- Subject / Assunto
- Message / Mensagem
- Consent checkbox linked to privacy notice

## 5. Content ownership labels

Use visible labels where ambiguity is possible:

- `HLC Mozambique`
- `CC1 — Technical Partner`
- `CC1 Technical Reference Project`
- `Source: HLC Institutional Profile`
- `Source: CC1 project page` for Vinh Tan 4

## 6. Initial content strategy

Do not launch empty News, Careers, Partners or Customers pages. Add them only when approved content exists. A smaller complete website is better than a larger site with placeholder claims.
