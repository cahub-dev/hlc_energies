## 6. CC1 publication-sensitive items

Separate approval is required for:

- CC1 logo;
- project photographs;
- financial indicators;
- ISO certification claims or certificate images;
- customer and partner logos;
- detailed project descriptions;
- use of the words “guarantees” and “financial capacity” in public marketing context;
- reproduction of content from the CC1 website.

## 8. Asset metadata record

Every public asset should record:

- file name;
- owner/source;
- licence or written permission;
- photographer/credit if required;
- project depicted;
- whether it is HLC, CC1 or illustrative;
- alt text in PT and EN;
- crop variants;
- approval date and approver.

## 9. Pre-build blockers

The build may use clearly marked placeholders, but production publication is blocked until:

- official HLC logo files are available;
- hero media rights are confirmed;
- CC1 image/logo permissions are confirmed or those assets are omitted;
- the contact mailbox is tested;
- company-profile download version is approved;
- legal/privacy text is approved.
