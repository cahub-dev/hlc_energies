# HLC Energies Website Brief

## 1. Objective

Create a credible bilingual institutional website for HLC Energias Renováveis e Infraestruturas, LDA at `hlcenergies.com`.

The website must explain:

- who HLC is;
- HLC's legal and operational role in Mozambique;
- the HLC–CC1 Consortium model;
- approved areas of interest;
- CC1's separately attributed technical capability and reference projects;
- HLC's long-term commitment to Mozambique;
- how stakeholders can contact HLC.

## 2. Business impact

The site should strengthen confidence among:

- Mozambican national and provincial authorities;
- energy-sector stakeholders;
- potential clients and project partners;
- investors and financiers reviewing company credibility;
- contractors, technical partners and national suppliers;
- prospective Mozambican employees and technical personnel.

Primary value: institutional credibility and business-development support. The website is not an e-commerce product and should not be optimized around consumer sales.

## 3. Positioning

HLC is presented as a Mozambican contracting and local-execution entity for energy, infrastructure and construction projects of the HLC Group and its technical partners in Mozambique.

For energy and infrastructure projects in Mozambique, HLC operates under a contractual Consortium with Construction Corporation No. 1 – JSC (CC1).

The site must communicate:

- **HLC:** local legal entity, contracting, licensing, workforce, local content, logistics and works support.
- **CC1:** technical/EPC capacity, engineering, project management, technical personnel, ISO systems, guarantees and financial capacity.

## 4. Design direction

Primary visual reference: `https://evostel.com/`.

Adopt:

- premium industrial presentation;
- full-screen hero media;
- floating pill navigation;
- large editorial typography;
- generous white space;
- restrained scroll reveals;
- horizontal capability/project cards;
- prominent brochure download;
- minimal contact experience.

Do not copy Evostel's text, brand palette, industries, statistics, media or partner claims.

## 5. Brand personality

- Institutional
- Technical
- Confident but factual
- Mozambican and locally committed
- Internationally capable through the CC1 consortium
- Premium but not extravagant
- Engineering-led, not startup-like

## 6. Required languages

- Portuguese: authoritative
- English: faithful translation

Language choice must be visible on desktop and mobile. URLs should be language-aware, preferably `/pt/` and `/en/`, with appropriate `hreflang` tags.

## 7. Version 1 scope

### Included

- Bilingual homepage
- About HLC
- Areas of Interest
- HLC–CC1 Consortium
- CC1 Technical Capacity
- CC1 Reference Projects
- Commitment to Mozambique / capacity transfer
- Contact page and form
- Company-profile download
- Legal/privacy pages
- Responsive navigation and footer
- Basic analytics only after approval

### Excluded unless approved

- News CMS
- Careers portal
- Customer login
- Live chat or chatbot
- Project tender portal
- Supplier registration workflow
- Investor portal
- E-commerce
- Unapproved social-media feeds
- Interactive global project map

## 8. Success criteria

- A visitor understands HLC's role within 15 seconds.
- A visitor can distinguish HLC from CC1 without ambiguity.
- Every CC1 reference is correctly attributed.
- Portuguese and English content are complete and consistent.
- Mobile pages remain fast on constrained connections.
- Contact submissions are delivered reliably without exposing credentials.
- The website passes the release gates in `07_QA_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md`.

## 9. Decision framework

- **Technical complexity:** Low to medium
- **Cost:** Low to medium
- **Primary risk:** Reputational/legal misattribution of CC1 experience
- **Recommendation:** Static-first bilingual website with a minimal content layer and no unnecessary platform complexity
- **Why:** It maximizes speed, security and performance while meeting the actual institutional objective.
