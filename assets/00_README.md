**Domain:** `hlcenergies.com`  
**Company:** HLC Energias Renováveis e Infraestruturas, LDA  
**Design direction:** Evostel-inspired visual and interaction system, adapted to HLC branding and approved content

**Languages:** Portuguese (authoritative) and English (translation)

## Purpose

This package gives design, content and development agents the controlled information required to build the HLC Energies website without inventing facts or misattributing CC1 experience.

If documents conflict on company facts, `COPY.md` prevails. If a fact is absent from `COPY.md`, the agent must not invent it.

## Non-negotiable content controls

- HLC and CC1 are separate entities with distinct roles.
- CC1 projects must never be presented as HLC-executed projects.
- CC1 statistics, certifications, clients and experience must not be attributed to HLC.
- Do not claim completed HLC projects in Mozambique.
- Do not create telephone numbers, social accounts, offices, partners, services or statistics.
- Portuguese is authoritative; English must remain a faithful translation.
- CC1 photographs and logos require permission before publication.
- Legal identifiers and representative details require final publication approval.

## Product principle

Build a simple, premium institutional website. Copy Evostel's clarity and interaction language, not its content, claims, statistics or colour palette.

## Recommended implementation priority

1. Static bilingual institutional website
2. Fast mobile performance
3. Approved project-reference presentation
4. Contact form with reliable delivery and anti-spam
5. Lightweight content maintenance

Do not add a complex CMS, investor portal, customer account system, live chat or news workflow in version 1 unless separately approved.
